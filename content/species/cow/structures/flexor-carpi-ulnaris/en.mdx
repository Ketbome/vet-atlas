## Overview

It has two heads, humeral and ulnar, that join in a common tendon.

It forms the caudomedial border of the forearm and ends on the accessory carpal bone.

## Origin

- Humeral head (*caput humerale*): medial epicondyle of the humerus.
- Ulnar head (*caput ulnare*): medial surface of the olecranon.

## Insertion

Accessory carpal bone (*os carpi accessorium*).

## Action

Flexes the carpus.

## Innervation

Ulnar nerve (*n. ulnaris*).
