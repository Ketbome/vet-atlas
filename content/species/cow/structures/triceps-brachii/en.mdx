---
title: Triceps brachii
summary: Large triangular muscle mass caudal to the humerus; the main extensor of the elbow.
---

## Overview

It fills the angle between the caudal border of the scapula and the humerus. In cattle it has three heads ending on the olecranon: long, lateral and medial. The long head is the bulkiest.

## Origin

- **Long head (*caput longum*)**: caudal border of the scapula. The only head that crosses the shoulder.
- **Lateral head (*caput laterale*)**: tricipital line on the lateral surface of the proximal humerus.
- **Medial head (*caput mediale*)**: medial surface of the humerus.

## Insertion

Olecranon tuber (*tuber olecrani*) of the ulna.

## Action

Extends the elbow and supports body weight when standing. The long head also flexes the shoulder.

## Innervation

Radial nerve (*n. radialis*).

## Clinical relevance

In **radial nerve paralysis**, from humeral fractures or compression during prolonged lateral recumbency, the triceps is inactive: the elbow drops, the animal cannot bear weight on the limb and knuckles over onto the dorsal surface of the hoof.
