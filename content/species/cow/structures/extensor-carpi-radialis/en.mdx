## Overview

The most cranial muscle of the forearm.

In cattle it forms a single belly with a single tendon. The tendon crosses the dorsal surface of the carpus wrapped in a synovial sheath.

## Origin

Lateral supracondylar crest of the humerus.

## Insertion

Tuberosity of the large metacarpal bone III+IV (*tuberositas ossis metacarpalis III*), on the dorsal surface of the proximal metacarpus.

## Action

Extends the carpus.

## Innervation

Radial nerve (*n. radialis*).

## Clinical relevance

Over the dorsal carpus, superficial to its tendon, a **carpal hygroma** can develop. It is an inflammation of a subcutaneous bursa, common in cattle housed on hard floors.

It must be told apart from distension of this tendon sheath and of the carpal joint.
