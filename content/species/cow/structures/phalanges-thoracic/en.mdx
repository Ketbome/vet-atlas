## Overview

Cattle bear weight on **two main digits**: **III**, medial, and **IV**, lateral. Each has **three phalanges**: proximal, middle and distal.

Digits **II and V** are the **dewclaws**, or accessory digits, on the palmar side of the fetlock. They contain one or two small bones that **do not articulate** with the rest of the skeleton.

Each digit has three joints:

- the **metacarpophalangeal**, or fetlock, joint
- the **proximal interphalangeal**, or pastern, joint
- the **distal interphalangeal** joint, inside the claw

In the thoracic limb the medial claw is usually slightly larger than the lateral.

## Phalanges

- **Proximal phalanx** (*phalanx proximalis*). The longest. Articulates with one condyle of the metacarpus and with the proximal sesamoid bones.
- **Middle phalanx** (*phalanx media*). Shorter. Its distal end lies partly inside the claw.
- **Distal phalanx** (*phalanx distalis*). Entirely enclosed in the **claw**, whose shape it mirrors.

The distal phalanx has:

- a **parietal surface**, under the horn wall
- a **solear surface**, over the sole
- an **axial surface**, facing the interdigital space
- the dorsal **extensor process**, for the tendon of the digital extensor
- the **flexor tubercle**, on the solear surface, for the tendon of the deep digital flexor

## Sesamoid bones

- **Proximal sesamoid bones** (*ossa sesamoidea proximalia*). **Four** per limb, two on the palmar side of each fetlock.
- **Distal sesamoid bone** (*os sesamoideum distale*). **One per digit**, palmar to the distal interphalangeal joint. The tendon of the deep digital flexor passes over it to the flexor tubercle.

## Clinical relevance

### Fractures of the distal phalanx

They mainly affect the **medial claw of the thoracic limb**, sometimes in both forefeet.

They are treated by gluing a **block** onto the sound claw to unload the injured one.

### Septic pedal osteitis and septic distal interphalangeal arthritis

Complications of claw lesions such as sole ulcer, white line disease or toe necrosis, when infection reaches the bone or the joint.

They may require resection of the affected tissue or amputation of the digit.

### Toe necrosis

Affects the tip of the distal phalanx.
