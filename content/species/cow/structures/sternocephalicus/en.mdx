## Overview

It arises on the manubrium of the sternum and runs along the ventrolateral neck to the head. In cattle it divides into two parts:

- **Sternomandibularis** (*pars mandibularis*). The more superficial. It ends on the mandible.
- **Sternomastoideus** (*pars mastoidea*). Deep. It ends in the region of the mastoid process.

## Origin

Manubrium of the sternum.

## Insertion

- Sternomandibularis: ventral border of the mandible, next to the rostral border of the masseter.
- Sternomastoideus: mastoid process of the temporal bone.

## Action

Draws the head and neck ventrally or to the same side.

## Innervation

Ventral branch of the accessory nerve (*n. accessorius*).

## Clinical relevance

It forms the **ventral border of the jugular groove**.

In cattle, the external jugular vein, which runs in this groove, is used for drawing blood and for intravenous medication. One example is calcium for periparturient hypocalcaemia, or milk fever.
