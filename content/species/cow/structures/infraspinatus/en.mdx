## Overview

It lies in the infraspinous fossa, caudal to the spine of the scapula. The deltoideus partly covers it.

Its belly is crossed by tendinous sheets. Before inserting, its tendon glides over a synovial bursa on the caudal part of the greater tubercle.

## Origin

Infraspinous fossa (*fossa infraspinata*) of the scapula.

## Insertion

Lateral surface of the caudal part of the greater tubercle of the humerus, distal to the supraspinatus insertion.

## Action

Abducts the arm and rotates it laterally. Depending on the position of the shoulder, it helps flex or extend it.

It also works as a **lateral collateral ligament** of the joint.

## Innervation

Suprascapular nerve (*n. suprascapularis*).

## Clinical relevance

Injury to the **suprascapular nerve** causes atrophy of the supraspinatus and infraspinatus, and the spine of the scapula becomes very prominent.
