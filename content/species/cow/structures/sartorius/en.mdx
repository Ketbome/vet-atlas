## Overview

In cattle, as in the horse, it arises inside the abdominal cavity, from the iliac fascia. It runs down the medial surface of the thigh to the stifle.

Its caudal border and the cranial border of the gracilis bound the **femoral triangle**, through which the femoral artery and vein run.

## Origin

Iliac fascia and the tendon of the psoas minor.

## Insertion

Medial side of the stifle, through the fascia, together with the gracilis.

## Action

Flexes the hip and adducts the pelvic limb.

## Innervation

Femoral nerve (*n. femoralis*), through the saphenous nerve.
