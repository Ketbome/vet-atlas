---
title: Gluteus medius
summary: Thick muscle of the croup from the ilium to the greater trochanter; the main extensor of the hip.
---

## Overview

The largest of the gluteal muscles. It covers the lateral surface of the wing of the ilium between the tuber coxae and the tuber sacrale, and is partly covered caudally by the gluteobiceps. In the carcass it is the top sirloin.

## Origin

Gluteal surface of the ilium, tuber coxae, sacroiliac ligament and gluteal fascia.

## Insertion

Greater trochanter of the femur (*trochanter major*).

## Action

Extends the hip and abducts the pelvic limb.

## Innervation

Cranial gluteal nerve (*n. gluteus cranialis*).

## Clinical relevance

- Carcass quality audits found injection-site lesions mainly in this muscle and in the round. For this reason Beef Quality Assurance guidelines call for injecting in the neck and not in the rump.
- In downer cows, prolonged recumbency can cause **ischaemic myopathy** in this and other large muscles of the pelvic limb.
