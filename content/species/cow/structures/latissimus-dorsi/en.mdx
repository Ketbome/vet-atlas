---
title: Latissimus dorsi
summary: Large triangular muscle of the thoracic wall that draws the thoracic limb caudally.
---

## Overview

A broad, flat muscle caudal to the scapula. Its fibres converge cranioventrally, pass medial to the long head of the triceps and end in a tendon shared with the teres major.

## Origin

Thoracolumbar fascia, which attaches it to the spinous processes of the caudal thoracic and lumbar vertebrae.

## Insertion

Teres major tuberosity (*tuberositas teres major*) on the medial surface of the humerus.

## Action

Flexes the shoulder and draws the limb caudally. With the limb planted, it pulls the trunk cranially.

## Innervation

Thoracodorsal nerve (*n. thoracodorsalis*).
