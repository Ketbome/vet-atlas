---
title: Semimembranosus
summary: Bulky muscle of the caudomedial thigh.
---

## Overview

It lies medial to the semitendinosus and divides into two bellies, one to the femur and one to the tibia. In the carcass it is the topside (inside round).

## Origin

Ischiatic tuberosity (*tuber ischiadicum*), on its ventral surface.

## Insertion

- Cranial belly: medial epicondyle of the femur.
- Caudal belly: medial condyle of the tibia.

## Action

Extends the hip and adducts the limb; depending on the position of the stifle it helps extend or flex it.

## Innervation

Sciatic nerve (*n. ischiadicus*).

## Clinical relevance

In **nutritional myodegeneration** (white muscle disease) of calves, caused by selenium and vitamin E deficiency, the large muscles of the pelvic limbs, such as this one, show pale streaks of necrosis, often symmetrical on both sides. The calf is stiff, weak or unable to rise.
