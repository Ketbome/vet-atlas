## Overview

Cattle bear weight on two digits per limb, **III** and **IV**.

Each ends in a **claw** (*ungula*), a horn capsule wrapping the distal phalanx. The two claws of a limb form the **cloven hoof** and are separated by the **interdigital space**, covered by skin.

Digits **II** and **V** are reduced to the **dewclaws** (*paraungulae*), caudal to the fetlock, which do not bear weight.

## Horn capsule

- **Periople**. Band of soft horn at the proximal edge, next to the haired skin.
- **Wall**. Hard horn forming the **dorsal** and **abaxial** surfaces and continuing on the **axial** surface, towards the interdigital space.
- **Sole**. Plate of horn on the ground surface, between the wall and the heel.
- **Heel**, or bulb. **Soft**, elastic horn on the palmar or plantar part. Beneath it a digital cushion of fatty tissue absorbs impact.
- **White line**. Junction between the wall and the sole. It is a weak point of the capsule.

## Dermis

Between bone and horn lies the **dermis**, or corium, highly vascular and innervated. It produces and nourishes the horn.

- At the **coronary band** its surface is **papillary** and forms most of the wall, which grows distally.
- Under the wall it is **laminar**. Its laminae interlock with those of the horn and attach the distal phalanx to the capsule.
- Under the sole and heel it is papillary again.

## Clinical relevance

Lameness is one of the main welfare problems and causes of economic loss in dairy cattle.

Most lesions occur in the **lateral claw of the pelvic limb**, which bears more weight.

### Lesions

- **Sole ulcer**. Lesion of the sole where it meets the heel, caused by compression of the corium between bone and horn.
- **White line disease**. Separation of the white line that lets dirt and infection in.
- **Digital dermatitis**, or Mortellaro disease. Lesion of the skin near the heels associated with *Treponema* spp., painful and contagious. It is controlled with footbaths.
- **Interdigital phlegmon**, or foot rot. Infection of the interdigital skin and tissue by *Fusobacterium necrophorum*, with symmetrical swelling and a foul smell.
- **Laminitis**. Inflammation of the corium, often **subclinical** and linked to **ruminal acidosis**. It produces poor-quality horn that predisposes to sole ulcer and white line disease.

### Hoof trimming

Regular functional trimming corrects overgrowth and shares weight between the two claws.
