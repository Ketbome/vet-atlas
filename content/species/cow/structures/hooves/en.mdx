## Overview

Cattle bear weight on two digits per limb, **III** and **IV**. Each ends in a **claw** (*ungula*), a horn capsule wrapping the distal phalanx; the two claws of a limb form the **cloven hoof** and are separated by the **interdigital space**, covered by skin. Digits **II** and **V** are reduced to the **dewclaws** (*paraungulae*), caudal to the fetlock, which do not bear weight.

## Horn capsule

- **Periople**: band of soft horn at the proximal edge, next to the haired skin.
- **Wall**: hard horn forming the **dorsal** and **abaxial** surfaces and continuing on the **axial** surface, towards the interdigital space.
- **Sole**: plate of horn on the ground surface, between the wall and the heel.
- **Heel (bulb)**: **soft**, elastic horn on the palmar or plantar part, with a digital cushion of fatty tissue beneath it that absorbs impact.
- **White line**: junction between the wall and the sole; it is a weak point of the capsule.

## Dermis (corium)

Between bone and horn lies the **dermis**, highly vascular and innervated, which produces and nourishes the horn. At the **coronary band** its surface is **papillary** and forms most of the wall, which grows distally. Under the wall it is **laminar**: its laminae interlock with those of the horn and attach the distal phalanx to the capsule. Under the sole and heel it is papillary again.

## Clinical relevance

Lameness is one of the main welfare problems and causes of economic loss in dairy cattle. Most lesions occur in the **lateral claw of the pelvic limb**, which bears more weight.

- **Sole ulcer**: lesion of the sole where it meets the heel, caused by compression of the corium between bone and horn.
- **White line disease**: separation of the white line that lets dirt and infection in.
- **Digital dermatitis (Mortellaro disease)**: lesion of the skin near the heels associated with *Treponema* spp., painful and contagious; it is controlled with footbaths.
- **Interdigital phlegmon (foot rot)**: infection of the interdigital skin and tissue by *Fusobacterium necrophorum*, with symmetrical swelling and a foul smell.
- **Laminitis**: inflammation of the corium, often **subclinical** and linked to **ruminal acidosis**; it produces poor-quality horn that predisposes to sole ulcer and white line disease.
- **Hoof trimming**: regular functional trimming corrects overgrowth and shares weight between the two claws.
