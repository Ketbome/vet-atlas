---
title: Deltoideus
summary: Muscle on the lateral side of the shoulder; flexes the shoulder and abducts the arm.
---

## Overview

It lies on the lateral surface of the scapula and shoulder, caudal to the spine, and covers part of the infraspinatus. In cattle it has two parts:

- **Scapular part (*pars scapularis*)**: arises through an aponeurosis from the spine of the scapula.
- **Acromial part (*pars acromialis*)**: shorter, arising from the acromion.

## Origin

Spine of the scapula (scapular part) and acromion (acromial part).

## Insertion

Deltoid tuberosity (*tuberositas deltoidea*) of the humerus.

## Action

Flexes the shoulder and abducts the arm.

## Innervation

Axillary nerve (*n. axillaris*).
