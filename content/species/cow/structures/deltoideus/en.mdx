## Overview

It lies on the lateral surface of the scapula and shoulder, caudal to the spine, and covers part of the infraspinatus.

In cattle it has two parts:

- **Scapular part** (*pars scapularis*). Arises through an aponeurosis from the spine of the scapula.
- **Acromial part** (*pars acromialis*). Shorter, arises from the acromion.

## Origin

- Scapular part: spine of the scapula.
- Acromial part: acromion.

## Insertion

Deltoid tuberosity (*tuberositas deltoidea*) of the humerus.

## Action

Flexes the shoulder and abducts the arm.

## Innervation

Axillary nerve (*n. axillaris*).
