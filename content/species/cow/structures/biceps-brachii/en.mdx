## Overview

It lies on the cranial surface of the humerus.

Its tendon of origin crosses the shoulder joint and runs in the **intertubercular groove** of the humerus. In cattle this is a single groove between the greater and lesser tubercles.

There the tendon glides over the **intertubercular bursa** (*bursa intertubercularis*).

The belly is crossed by internal tendinous sheets.

## Origin

Supraglenoid tubercle of the scapula (*tuberculum supraglenoidale*).

## Insertion

Radial tuberosity (*tuberositas radii*), on the medial side of the proximal forearm.

## Action

Flexes the elbow and extends the shoulder.

When standing it helps fix the shoulder joint.

## Innervation

Musculocutaneous nerve (*n. musculocutaneus*).
