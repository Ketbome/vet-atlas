---
title: Supraspinatus
summary: Fills the supraspinous fossa of the scapula and extends the shoulder.
---

## Overview

A thick muscle that fills the supraspinous fossa and bulges beyond the cranial border of the scapula. Together with the infraspinatus it acts as a contractile ligament stabilising the shoulder joint. In cattle it ends in two branches, one on each side of the biceps brachii tendon.

## Origin

Supraspinous fossa (*fossa supraspinata*) and spine of the scapula.

## Insertion

Cranial parts of the greater tubercle (*tuberculum majus*) and the lesser tubercle (*tuberculum minus*) of the humerus.

## Action

Extends and stabilises the shoulder.

## Innervation

Suprascapular nerve (*n. suprascapularis*).

## Clinical relevance

- Suprascapular nerve injury causes atrophy of the supraspinatus and infraspinatus, leaving the spine of the scapula very prominent.
- Cranial to its border, above the shoulder joint, the **superficial cervical (prescapular) lymph nodes** are palpated.
