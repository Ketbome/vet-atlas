## Overview

A thick muscle that fills the supraspinous fossa and bulges beyond the cranial border of the scapula.

Together with the infraspinatus it acts as a **contractile ligament** that stabilises the shoulder joint.

In cattle it ends in **two branches**, one on each side of the biceps brachii tendon.

## Origin

Supraspinous fossa (*fossa supraspinata*) and spine of the scapula.

## Insertion

Cranial parts of the greater tubercle (*tuberculum majus*) and the lesser tubercle (*tuberculum minus*) of the humerus.

## Action

Extends and stabilises the shoulder.

## Innervation

Suprascapular nerve (*n. suprascapularis*).

## Clinical relevance

- Injury to the **suprascapular nerve** causes atrophy of the supraspinatus and infraspinatus, and the spine of the scapula becomes very prominent.
- Cranial to its border, above the shoulder joint, the **superficial cervical lymph nodes**, or prescapular nodes, are palpated.
