## Overview

It arises on the caudal surface of the proximal humerus and spirals down the **brachial groove** (*sulcus musculi brachialis*) to the cranial side of the elbow.

In cattle, as in the other ungulates, it ends on the radius.

## Origin

Caudal surface of the proximal humerus, distal to the head.

## Insertion

Proximal end of the radius, on its medial side.

## Action

Flexes the elbow.

## Innervation

Musculocutaneous nerve (*n. musculocutaneus*).

## Clinical relevance

The **radial nerve** accompanies this muscle in the brachial groove and crosses the lateral side of the arm.

It can be injured by:

- fractures of the humeral shaft
- compression during prolonged lateral recumbency, in downer cows or surgery in lateral recumbency

The animal cannot extend the elbow, carpus or digits.
