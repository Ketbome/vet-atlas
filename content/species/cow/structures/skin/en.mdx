## Overview

The skin (*cutis*) covers the whole body surface and is continuous with the mucous membranes at the natural openings.

It works as a physical, chemical and microbiological barrier. It takes part in thermoregulation and in the perception of touch, temperature, pain and itch.

In cattle it is **thick**, especially over the dorsal neck and trunk.

Beneath it, the **cutaneous trunci muscle** (*m. cutaneus trunci*) lets the animal twitch the skin to shake off insects.

## Layers

- **Epidermis**. Keratinised stratified squamous epithelium without blood vessels. It is thicker on the muzzle and the skin of the digits.
- **Dermis**. Dense connective tissue with collagen and elastic fibres. It holds vessels, nerves, hair follicles, arrector pili muscles and glands. This layer gives bovine skin its thickness.
- **Hypodermis** (*tela subcutanea*). Loose connective tissue with fat.

## Hair and glands

- **Coat**. **Simple** hair follicles, with a single hair per opening. Coat length and density vary with breed and season.
- **Sweat glands**. Apocrine, associated with the hair follicles. Unlike the dog and cat, cattle lose heat by sweating as well as by breathing.
- **Sebaceous glands**. Open into the follicles and lubricate hair and skin.
- **Dewlap**. Fold of skin on the ventral neck, very well developed in zebu cattle (*Bos indicus*).

## Specialised regions

- **Muzzle** (*planum nasolabiale*). Hairless area between the nostrils and the upper lip. The **nasolabial glands** in its dermis keep it moist and cool. Its pattern of grooves and ridges is unique to each animal and can be used to identify it.
- **Udder, horns and hooves**. Modified skin structures described separately.

## Clinical relevance

### Dermatophytosis, or ringworm

Infection by *Trichophyton verrucosum*, mainly in calves. It causes circular lesions with greyish crusts, often around the eyes.

It is a **zoonosis**.

### Lumpy skin disease

Disease caused by a capripoxvirus and spread by insects. It causes firm skin nodules and fever.

It is **notifiable**.

### Papillomatosis

Warts caused by bovine papillomavirus (BPV), common in young animals, on the head and neck.

### Dermatophilosis

Infection by *Dermatophilus congolensis*, favoured by prolonged wetting. It forms thick crusts that pull out tufts of hair.

### Ectoparasites

Lice and mange:

- *Chorioptes* mainly affects the tail base, perineum and limbs.
- *Psoroptes* causes extensive, very itchy lesions on the body.
- *Sarcoptes* can also affect people.

### Photosensitisation

Dermatitis of unpigmented skin exposed to sunlight. It is caused by photodynamic plant substances or, in the hepatogenous form, by liver damage that prevents phylloerythrin from being cleared.
