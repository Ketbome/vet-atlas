---
title: Semitendinosus
summary: Long muscle forming the caudal border of the thigh, between the biceps femoris and the semimembranosus.
---

## Overview

In cattle it arises only from the ischiatic tuberosity. It runs down the caudal border of the thigh and ends on the medial side of the leg; a tarsal tendon joins it to the common calcanean tendon. In the carcass it is the eye of round.

## Origin

Ischiatic tuberosity (*tuber ischiadicum*).

## Insertion

Medial surface of the proximal tibia (tibial crest) and, through the tarsal tendon, the calcaneal tuber.

## Action

Extends the hip, flexes the stifle and helps extend the tarsus.

## Innervation

Sciatic nerve (*n. ischiadicus*).

## Clinical relevance

- It is one of the valuable cuts of the hindquarter: beef quality guidelines call for no injections in this region and for injecting in the neck instead.
- In downer cows, prolonged compression of the caudal thigh muscles causes **ischaemic myopathy** and can injure the sciatic nerve.
