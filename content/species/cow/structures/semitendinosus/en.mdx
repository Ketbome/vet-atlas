## Overview

In cattle it arises only from the ischiatic tuberosity. It runs down the caudal border of the thigh and ends on the medial side of the leg.

A **tarsal tendon** joins it to the common calcanean tendon.

In the carcass it is the eye of round.

## Origin

Ischiatic tuberosity (*tuber ischiadicum*).

## Insertion

Medial surface of the proximal tibia, at the tibial crest, and, through the tarsal tendon, the calcaneal tuber.

## Action

Extends the hip, flexes the stifle and helps extend the tarsus.

## Innervation

Sciatic nerve (*n. ischiadicus*).

## Clinical relevance

### Injections

It is one of the valuable cuts of the hindquarter. Beef quality guidelines call for no injections in this region and for injecting in the neck instead.

### Downer cows

Prolonged compression of the caudal thigh muscles causes **ischaemic myopathy** and can injure the sciatic nerve.
