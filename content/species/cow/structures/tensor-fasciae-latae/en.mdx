---
title: Tensor fasciae latae
summary: Large muscle of the craniolateral thigh that arises from the tuber coxae and tenses the fascia lata.
---

## Overview

In cattle it is a large, fan-shaped muscle. Its fleshy part arises from the tuber coxae (the "hook" or point of the hip), fills the angle between the gluteus medius and the quadriceps and continues into the **fascia lata**, the thick lateral fascia of the thigh that reaches the patella.

## Origin

Tuber coxae (*tuber coxae*).

## Insertion

Fascia lata and, through it, the patella.

## Action

Flexes the hip and extends the stifle by tensing the fascia lata.

## Innervation

Cranial gluteal nerve (*n. gluteus cranialis*).
