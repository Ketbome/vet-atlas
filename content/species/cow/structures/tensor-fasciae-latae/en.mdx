## Overview

In cattle it is a large, fan-shaped muscle.

Its fleshy part arises from the tuber coxae and fills the angle between the gluteus medius and the quadriceps.

It continues into the **fascia lata**, the thick lateral fascia of the thigh, which reaches the patella.

## Origin

Tuber coxae (*tuber coxae*).

## Insertion

Fascia lata and, through it, the patella.

## Action

Flexes the hip and extends the stifle by tensing the fascia lata.

## Innervation

Cranial gluteal nerve (*n. gluteus cranialis*).
