## Overview

It is enclosed in the rectus sheath, formed by the aponeuroses of the oblique and transverse abdominal muscles.

The two recti are separated by the linea alba. Several transverse **tendinous intersections** cross it.

In the cow, the **subcutaneous abdominal vein**, or "milk vein", runs on each side, superficial to the ventral abdominal wall and lateral to the midline. It drains the udder and passes through the wall at the "milk well".

## Origin

Sternum and costal cartilages.

## Insertion

Pecten of the pubis, through the prepubic tendon.

## Action

Compresses the abdomen and flexes the vertebral column.

## Innervation

Ventral branches of the caudal thoracic and cranial lumbar nerves.

## Clinical relevance

### Right paramedian abomasopexy

With the cow in dorsal recumbency, it fixes the displaced abomasum to the ventral abdominal wall, to the right of the midline, through the rectus region.

The subcutaneous abdominal vein must be avoided.

### Umbilical hernia

In calves it occurs through the umbilical ring, in the linea alba between the two recti.
