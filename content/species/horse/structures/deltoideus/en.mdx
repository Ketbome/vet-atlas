---
title: Deltoideus
summary: Muscle on the lateral surface of the shoulder; flexes the shoulder and abducts the arm.
---

## Overview

It lies on the lateral surface of the scapula and shoulder, caudal to the spine, and covers part of the infraspinatus. The horse **has no acromion**: the scapular spine ends distally without that prominence. The deltoideus therefore has no separate acromial part as in cattle; it arises almost entirely from an aponeurosis attached to the spine and the caudal border of the scapula.

## Origin

Spine of the scapula, through an aponeurosis, and caudal border of the scapula.

## Insertion

Deltoid tuberosity (*tuberositas deltoidea*) of the humerus, very prominent in the horse.

## Action

Flexes the shoulder and abducts the arm.

## Innervation

Axillary nerve (*n. axillaris*).
