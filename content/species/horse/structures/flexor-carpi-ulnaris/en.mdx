## Overview

It has two heads, humeral and ulnar, that join in a common tendon.

It forms the caudomedial border of the forearm, superficial to the digital flexors, and ends on the accessory carpal bone.

## Origin

- Humeral head (*caput humerale*), on the medial epicondyle of the humerus.
- Ulnar head (*caput ulnare*), on the medial surface of the olecranon.

## Insertion

Accessory carpal bone (*os carpi accessorium*).

## Action

Flexes the carpus.

## Innervation

Ulnar nerve (*n. ulnaris*).

## Clinical relevance

The ulnaris lateralis (*m. ulnaris lateralis*, formerly the extensor carpi ulnaris) also ends on the accessory carpal bone. Both muscles pull on that bone.

In **accessory carpal bone fractures** this pull distracts the fragments. As a result they usually heal by fibrous rather than bony union.
