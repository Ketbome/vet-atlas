---
title: Flexor carpi ulnaris
summary: Carpal flexor on the caudal border of the forearm.
---

## Overview

It has two heads, humeral and ulnar, that join in a common tendon. It forms the caudomedial border of the forearm, superficial to the digital flexors, and ends on the accessory carpal bone.

## Origin

- Humeral head (*caput humerale*): medial epicondyle of the humerus.
- Ulnar head (*caput ulnare*): medial surface of the olecranon.

## Insertion

Accessory carpal bone (*os carpi accessorium*).

## Action

Flexes the carpus.

## Innervation

Ulnar nerve (*n. ulnaris*).

## Clinical relevance

Together with the ulnaris lateralis (*m. ulnaris lateralis*, formerly the extensor carpi ulnaris), which also ends on the accessory carpal bone, it pulls on that bone. In **accessory carpal bone fractures** this pull distracts the fragments, so they usually heal by fibrous rather than bony union.
