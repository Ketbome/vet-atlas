## Overview

In the horse it arises inside the abdominal cavity, from the iliac fascia. It runs down the medial thigh to the stifle.

Its caudal border and the cranial border of the gracilis bound the **femoral triangle**. The femoral artery and vein and the saphenous nerve run through it.

## Origin

Iliac fascia and tendon of the psoas minor.

## Insertion

Medial patellar ligament and medial surface of the proximal tibia, through the fascia, together with the gracilis.

## Action

Flexes the hip and adducts the pelvic limb.

## Innervation

Femoral nerve (*n. femoralis*), through the saphenous nerve.
