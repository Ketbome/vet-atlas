## Overview

It has a lateral and a medial head. The superficial digital flexor arises between them.

Its bellies contain much tendinous tissue and continue, at mid-crus, into a strong tendon. The horse also has a small **soleus** (*m. soleus*) that joins the gastrocnemius tendon.

The **common calcanean tendon** ("Achilles tendon") is made up of:

- the gastrocnemius tendon
- the superficial digital flexor tendon
- the tarsal tendons of the biceps femoris and semitendinosus

## Origin

Lateral and medial supracondylar tuberosities of the femur.

## Insertion

Calcaneal tuber (*tuber calcanei*).

## Action

Extends the hock and flexes the stifle. It is the main driver of the hock during propulsion.

## Innervation

Tibial nerve (*n. tibialis*).

## Clinical relevance

- **Rupture**. It affects the muscle or its tendon and is uncommon. It is described after falls, violent effort or a rough recovery from anaesthesia. The hock drops and flexes when the limb bears weight.
- **Tibial nerve**. It runs between the two heads of the gastrocnemius and on the caudal crus. It is blocked proximal to the hock in diagnostic anaesthesia of the pelvic limb.
