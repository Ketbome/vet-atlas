## Overview

It arises on the caudal surface of the proximal humerus and spirals down the **brachial groove** (*sulcus musculi brachialis*) to the cranial side of the elbow.

There it passes under the biceps tendon and ends on the medial surface of the radius.

## Origin

Caudal surface of the proximal humerus, distal to the head.

## Insertion

Proximal end of the radius, on its medial surface.

## Action

Flexes the elbow.

## Innervation

Musculocutaneous nerve (*n. musculocutaneus*).

## Clinical relevance

The **radial nerve** accompanies this muscle in the brachial groove and crosses the lateral surface of the arm.

It can be injured in two settings:

- fractures of the humeral shaft
- compression during general anaesthesia in lateral recumbency

The horse shows a **dropped elbow**. It cannot extend the elbow, carpus or fetlock and bears weight poorly on the hoof.
