## Overview

Broad, flat muscle caudal to the scapula. Its dorsal border lies under the bearing surface of the saddle.

Its fibres converge cranioventrally and pass medial to the long head of the triceps. They end in a common tendon with the teres major.

## Origin

Thoracolumbar fascia, which attaches it to the spinous processes of the caudal thoracic and lumbar vertebrae.

## Insertion

Teres major tuberosity (*tuberositas teres major*), on the medial surface of the humerus.

## Action

Flexes the shoulder and draws the limb caudally.

With the limb planted, it pulls the trunk cranially.

## Innervation

Thoracodorsal nerve (*n. thoracodorsalis*).
