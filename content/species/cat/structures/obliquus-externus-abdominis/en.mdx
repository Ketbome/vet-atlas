## Overview

It has a costal and a lumbar part.

Its fleshy part covers the lateral abdominal wall and the caudoventral thorax. Ventrally it continues as an aponeurosis that reaches the linea alba. The model shows the fleshy part.

## Origin

- **Costal part**. Lateral surfaces of the caudal ribs, by digitations alternating with those of the serratus ventralis.
- **Lumbar part**. Thoracolumbar fascia.

## Insertion

Through its aponeurosis, on the linea alba, the prepubic tendon and the inguinal ligament.

## Action

- Compresses the abdomen in expiration, defecation, urination and parturition.
- Bends the trunk to the same side.

## Innervation

Ventral branches of the caudal thoracic and cranial lumbar nerves.

## Clinical relevance

Its aponeurosis contains the **superficial inguinal ring**. Abdominal wall ruptures from road accidents or bite wounds can tear it.
