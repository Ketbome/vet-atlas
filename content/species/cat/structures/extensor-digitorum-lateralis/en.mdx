title: Extensor digitorum lateralis
summary: Digital extensor on the lateral surface of the forearm, caudal to the common digital extensor.
keyPoints:
  - It arises from the **lateral epicondyle** of the humerus.
  - Its tendons reach the **lateral digits**.
  - It **extends the digits** and the carpus.
  - It is supplied by the **radial nerve**.
---

## Overview

It lies between the common digital extensor (cranially) and the extensor carpi ulnaris (caudally). Its tendon crosses the dorsolateral surface of the carpus and divides for the lateral digits.

## Origin

Lateral epicondyle of the humerus.

## Insertion

Phalanges of the lateral digits, together with the tendons of the common digital extensor.

## Action

Extends the joints of the lateral digits and the carpus.

## Innervation

Radial nerve (*n. radialis*).
