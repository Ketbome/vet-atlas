---
title: Semitendinosus
summary: Long muscle forming the caudal border of the thigh, between the biceps femoris and the semimembranosus.
---

## Origin

Ischiatic tuberosity (*tuber ischiadicum*).

## Insertion

Medial surface of the proximal tibia.

## Action

Extends the hip and flexes the stifle.

## Innervation

Sciatic nerve (*n. ischiadicus*).

## Clinical relevance

- The sciatic nerve runs between the biceps femoris and the caudal thigh muscles. Intramuscular injections into this region are not recommended in the cat.
- Fibrotic myopathy of this muscle, typical of German Shepherd dogs, is only exceptionally reported in the cat.
