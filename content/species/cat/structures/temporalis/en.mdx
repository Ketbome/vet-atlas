---
title: Temporalis
summary: Powerful masticatory muscle that fills the temporal fossa and closes the mouth.
---

## Overview

As in other carnivores, it is the largest of the masticatory muscles. It covers the lateral surface of the cranial vault and passes medial to the zygomatic arch to the mandible.

## Origin

Temporal fossa, external sagittal crest and nuchal crest.

## Insertion

Coronoid process of the mandible.

## Action

Elevates the mandible and closes the mouth.

## Innervation

Mandibular nerve (*n. mandibularis*), a branch of the trigeminal nerve (cranial nerve V).

## Clinical relevance

It is palpated over the skull for muscle condition scoring. Its atrophy is common in older cats and in chronic diseases such as chronic kidney disease, hyperthyroidism or neoplasia.
