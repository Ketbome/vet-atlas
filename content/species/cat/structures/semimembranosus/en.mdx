## Overview

It lies medial to the semitendinosus. It divides into two bellies, one to the femur and one to the tibia.

## Origin

Ischiatic tuberosity (*tuber ischiadicum*) and ramus of the ischium.

## Insertion

- Cranial belly: medial epicondyle of the femur.
- Caudal belly: medial condyle of the tibia.

## Action

Extends the hip and adducts the limb.

Depending on the position of the stifle, it helps extend or flex it.

## Innervation

Sciatic nerve (*n. ischiadicus*).
