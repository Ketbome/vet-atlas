## Overview

A broad, flat muscle caudal to the scapula. Its fibres converge cranioventrally and pass medial to the long head of the triceps. They end in a tendon shared with the teres major.

In the cat, the epitrochlearis (*m. tensor fasciae antebrachii*) leaves its deep surface and ventral border. This muscle accompanies the triceps to the olecranon.

## Origin

Thoracolumbar fascia, which attaches it to the spinous processes of the caudal thoracic and lumbar vertebrae.

## Insertion

Teres major tuberosity (*tuberositas teres major*), on the medial surface of the humerus.

## Action

- Flexes the shoulder and draws the limb caudally.
- With the limb planted, it pulls the trunk cranially, as when climbing.

## Innervation

Thoracodorsal nerve (*n. thoracodorsalis*).

## Clinical relevance

- Feline **injection-site sarcoma** on the lateral thoracic wall or in the interscapular region can infiltrate this muscle. It then has to be partly removed.
- Its size and constant vascular pedicle make it a useful **muscle flap** for reconstructing defects of the thoracic wall, including those left by resection of these sarcomas.
