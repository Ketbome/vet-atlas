## Overview

The hip bone (*os coxae*) results from the fusion of three bones, the **ilium**, **ischium** and **pubis**, which meet at the **acetabulum**.

- Ventrally, the two hip bones meet at the **pelvic symphysis**.
- Dorsally, they articulate with the sacrum at the **sacroiliac joints**.

In the cat the pelvis is long and narrow, with its axis almost parallel to the spine.

## Key features

- **Ilium**. The cranial part. Its wing is narrow and elongated, with the palpable **iliac crest** and the auricular surface, which articulates with the sacrum. The gluteal muscles arise from its lateral surface.
- **Ischium**. The caudal part. It carries the **ischiatic tuberosity**, palpable on each side of the tail base, where the hamstring muscles arise: biceps femoris, semitendinosus and semimembranosus. The ischiatic arch lies between the two ischia.
- **Pubis**. The ventral and medial part. It forms the cranial edge of the obturator foramen and the cranial part of the symphysis.
- **Acetabulum**. Deep socket that receives the femoral head. Its articular surface is crescent-shaped (*facies lunata*) and opens ventrally at the acetabular notch. It surrounds the acetabular fossa, where the ligament of the femoral head attaches.
- **Obturator foramen**. Large opening between the pubis and ischium, closed in life by a membrane and the obturator muscles.

## Clinical relevance

### Pelvic fractures

They are among the most common fractures of the cat, nearly always from road accidents.

- They are usually multiple: the pelvic ring breaks in at least two places.
- They often come with sacroiliac luxation.
- Displaced fractures of the acetabulum or ilium usually need surgery.

Malunion reduces the diameter of the pelvic canal and can cause chronic constipation and **megacolon**.

In pelvic trauma the urethra, bladder and the sciatic and obturator nerves must be assessed.

### Hip dysplasia

In the cat it shows mainly as a **shallow acetabulum** with remodelling of its craniodorsal rim. The Maine Coon is predisposed.

Clinical signs are usually mild.
