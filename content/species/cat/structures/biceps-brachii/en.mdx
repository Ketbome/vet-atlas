---
title: Biceps brachii
summary: Spindle-shaped muscle on the cranial surface of the arm; flexes the elbow.
---

## Overview

In the cat it has a single belly. Its tendon of origin crosses the shoulder joint and runs in the intertubercular groove of the humerus, held by the transverse humeral retinaculum and wrapped in an extension of the joint capsule.

## Origin

Supraglenoid tubercle of the scapula (*tuberculum supraglenoidale*).

## Insertion

Radial tuberosity (*tuberositas radii*), on the medial side of the proximal forearm.

## Action

Flexes the elbow and extends the shoulder.

## Innervation

Musculocutaneous nerve (*n. musculocutaneus*).

## Clinical relevance

- The biceps reflex tests the musculocutaneous nerve and spinal cord segments C6–C8. In the cat it is often hard to elicit, so its absence alone means little.
- In brachial plexus avulsion after a road accident, elbow flexion is lost when the cranial roots (C6–C7) are affected.
