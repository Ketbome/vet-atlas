---
title: Flexor carpi radialis
summary: Flexor of the carpus on the medial surface of the forearm.
keyPoints:
  - It arises from the **medial epicondyle** of the humerus.
  - Its tendon ends on the palmar surface of the **second and third metacarpals**.
  - It **flexes the carpus**.
  - It is supplied by the **median nerve**.
---

## Overview

It lies on the medial surface of the forearm, caudal to the pronator teres and the radius. Its tendon crosses the medial side of the carpus.

## Origin

Medial epicondyle of the humerus.

## Insertion

Palmar surface of the bases of the second and third metacarpals.

## Action

Flexes the carpus.

## Innervation

Median nerve (*n. medianus*).
