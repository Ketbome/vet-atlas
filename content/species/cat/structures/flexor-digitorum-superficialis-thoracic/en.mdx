## Overview

It lies superficially on the caudal (palmar) surface of the forearm, between the flexor carpi radialis and the flexor carpi ulnaris, over the deep digital flexor. In the cat its superficial part is often described as a separate muscle, the palmaris longus.

## Origin

Medial epicondyle of the humerus.

## Insertion

Palmar surface of the digits, by tendons that cross the palmar side of the carpus.

## Action

Flexes the digits and the carpus.

## Innervation

Median nerve (*n. medianus*).
