---
title: Fibularis brevis
summary: Small muscle of the distal lateral crus, ending on the fifth metatarsal.
keyPoints:
  - It arises from the **distal half of the fibula** and tibia.
  - Its tendon passes behind the **lateral malleolus**.
  - It ends on the base of the **fifth metatarsal**.
  - It **flexes the tarsus**; it is supplied by the fibular nerve.
---

## Overview

It lies deep on the lateral surface of the distal crus, under the tendons of the fibularis longus and the lateral digital extensors.

## Origin

Lateral surfaces of the distal half of the fibula and tibia.

## Insertion

Base of the fifth metatarsal.

## Action

Flexes the tarsus.

## Innervation

Common fibular (peroneal) nerve (*n. fibularis communis*).
