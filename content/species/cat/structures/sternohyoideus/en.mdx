---
title: Sternohyoideus
summary: Long, thin strap on the ventral surface of the neck, next to its partner on the midline.
keyPoints:
  - It runs from the **manubrium** of the sternum to the basihyoid bone.
  - It lies on the ventral surface of the **trachea**.
  - It draws the hyoid and the tongue **caudally**.
  - The two muscles are parted on the midline to reach the **trachea** or the thyroid gland.
---

## Overview

The left and right muscles lie side by side on the ventral midline of the neck, over the trachea. The sternothyroideus runs lateral to them.

## Origin

Manubrium of the sternum and first costal cartilage.

## Insertion

Basihyoid bone.

## Action

Draws the hyoid apparatus and the root of the tongue caudally, as in swallowing.

## Innervation

Ventral branches of the first cervical nerves.

## Clinical relevance

In a ventral approach to the neck, for example to the trachea or the thyroid gland, the two muscles are separated along the midline.
