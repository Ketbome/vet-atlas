---
title: Brachioradialis
summary: Thin strap on the craniolateral surface of the forearm, well developed in the cat.
keyPoints:
  - It runs from the **lateral supracondylar crest** of the humerus to the distal radius.
  - It is the most superficial muscle on the **cranial surface of the forearm**.
  - It **supinates** the forearm, turning the palm of the paw medially.
  - It is supplied by the **radial nerve**.
---

## Overview

A narrow, ribbon-like muscle lying on the extensor carpi radialis. The cat uses supination much more than the dog, for instance when it handles prey; in the dog the brachioradialis is often vestigial or absent.

## Origin

Lateral supracondylar crest of the humerus, proximal to the extensor carpi radialis.

## Insertion

Medial side of the distal end of the radius.

## Action

Supinates the forearm.

## Innervation

Radial nerve (*n. radialis*).
