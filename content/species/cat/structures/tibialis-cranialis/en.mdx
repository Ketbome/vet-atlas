---
title: Tibialis cranialis
summary: The most cranial muscle of the leg; flexes the tarsus.
---

## Origin

Proximal end of the tibia (cranial border and lateral surface), head of the fibula and interosseous membrane.

## Insertion

Medial side of the distal tarsus, on the rudimentary metatarsal bone I.

## Action

Flexes the tarsus.

## Innervation

Common fibular (peroneal) nerve (*n. fibularis communis*).

## Clinical relevance

- In the neurological examination its belly is tapped to elicit tarsal flexion (cranial tibial reflex), which tests the fibular nerve.
- In feline aortic thromboembolism this muscle and the gastrocnemius become firm and painful from ischaemia.
