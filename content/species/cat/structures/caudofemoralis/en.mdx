## Overview

A strap-shaped muscle on the lateral surface of the thigh, caudal to the gluteus superficialis and partly covered by the biceps femoris. Its belly narrows into a long tendon that runs distally along the thigh to the stifle.

## Origin

Transverse processes of the first caudal vertebrae.

## Insertion

Lateral border of the patella, by a long tendon.

## Action

Abducts the thigh and extends the hip.

## Innervation

Caudal gluteal nerve (*n. gluteus caudalis*).
