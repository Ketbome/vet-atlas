---
title: Gluteus medius
summary: Thick muscle of the croup from the wing of the ilium to the greater trochanter; the main extensor of the hip.
---

## Overview

The largest of the gluteal muscles. It covers the lateral surface of the wing of the ilium and is partly covered caudally by the gluteus superficialis.

## Origin

Gluteal surface and crest of the ilium, and the gluteal fascia.

## Insertion

Greater trochanter of the femur (*trochanter major*).

## Action

Extends the hip and abducts the pelvic limb.

## Innervation

Cranial gluteal nerve (*n. gluteus cranialis*).
