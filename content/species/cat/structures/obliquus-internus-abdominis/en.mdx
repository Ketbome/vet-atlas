## Overview

It forms the middle of the three layers of the lateral abdominal wall, between the external oblique and the transversus abdominis. Its fleshy part lies in the flank; ventrally it continues as an aponeurosis that joins the sheath of the rectus abdominis.

## Origin

Tuber coxae, inguinal ligament and thoracolumbar fascia.

## Insertion

Last rib and costal arch, and through its aponeurosis the linea alba.

## Action

Compresses the abdomen, as in expiration, defecation and parturition. Acting on one side, it bends the trunk laterally.

## Innervation

Ventral branches of the last thoracic and the lumbar nerves.

## Clinical relevance

In a flank approach to the abdomen it is split along its fibres after the external oblique, and before the transversus abdominis.
