---
title: Masseter
summary: Thick masticatory muscle of the cheek, between the zygomatic arch and the mandible.
---

## Overview

The cat's short, broad face leaves room for a thick masseter. The feline temporomandibular joint allows almost only hinge movement, with very little lateral shift.

## Origin

Ventral border and medial surface of the zygomatic arch.

## Insertion

Masseteric fossa and ventral border of the caudal part of the mandible.

## Action

Elevates the mandible and closes the mouth.

## Innervation

Masseteric nerve, from the mandibular nerve (cranial nerve V).

## Clinical relevance

Atrophy limited to one side suggests a lesion of the mandibular nerve on that side.
