---
title: Anconeus
summary: Small muscle covering the caudolateral side of the elbow joint.
keyPoints:
  - It runs from the distal **humerus** to the lateral surface of the **olecranon**.
  - It covers the **olecranon fossa** and the joint capsule.
  - It **extends the elbow** with the triceps.
  - It is supplied by the **radial nerve**.
---

## Overview

A short, flat muscle that fills the space between the lateral epicondylar crest and the olecranon, partly covered by the lateral head of the triceps.

## Origin

Lateral epicondylar crest and the margins of the olecranon fossa of the humerus.

## Insertion

Lateral surface of the olecranon.

## Action

Extends the elbow and tenses its joint capsule.

## Innervation

Radial nerve (*n. radialis*).
