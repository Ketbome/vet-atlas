  - It runs from the **paracondylar process** of the skull to the ventral border of the mandible.

  - Its rostral part is supplied by the **mandibular nerve** (V), its caudal part by the **facial nerve** (VII).

## Overview

It lies medial to the angle and the ventral border of the mandible. In carnivores the two bellies of the muscle are fused into a single fleshy belly, with at most a faint tendinous line between them.

## Origin

Paracondylar (jugular) process of the occipital bone.

## Insertion

Ventromedial border of the body of the mandible.

## Action

Lowers the mandible and opens the mouth.

## Innervation

- **Rostral belly**. Mandibular nerve (cranial nerve V).
- **Caudal belly**. Facial nerve (cranial nerve VII).
