## Overview

A thick muscle on the dorsolateral surface of the first two vertebrae. Its fibres run craniolaterally, from the long spinous process of the axis to the wing of the atlas.

## Origin

Lateral surface of the spinous process of the axis.

## Insertion

Dorsal surface of the wing of the atlas.

## Action

Turns the atlas, and with it the head, about the dens of the axis.

## Innervation

Dorsal branches of the first cervical nerves.
