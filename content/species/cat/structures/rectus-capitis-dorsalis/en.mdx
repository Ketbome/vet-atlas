---
title: Rectus capitis dorsalis
summary: Short muscles on either side of the midline between the axis, the atlas and the occipital bone.
keyPoints:
  - The major part runs from the **spinous process of the axis** to the occipital bone.
  - Deeper, smaller parts run from the **dorsal arch of the atlas**.
  - They **extend** the head.
  - Cerebrospinal fluid is collected from the **cerebellomedullary cistern**, on the midline between the occiput and the atlas.
---

## Overview

Short muscles on each side of the dorsal midline, over the atlanto-occipital joint. They lie deep to the semispinalis capitis.

## Origin

Spinous process of the axis and dorsal arch of the atlas.

## Insertion

Occipital bone, ventral to the nuchal crest.

## Action

Extend the atlanto-occipital joint.

## Innervation

Dorsal branch of the first cervical nerve.

## Clinical relevance

For cerebrospinal fluid collection from the cerebellomedullary cistern, the needle is passed on the dorsal midline between the occipital bone and the atlas, between the muscles of the two sides.
