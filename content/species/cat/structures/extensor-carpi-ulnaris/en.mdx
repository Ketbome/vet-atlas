## Overview

It is the most caudal muscle of the lateral (extensor) group of the forearm and borders the flexor carpi ulnaris. Veterinary texts on the dog also call it the ulnaris lateralis (*m. ulnaris lateralis*).

## Origin

Lateral epicondyle of the humerus.

## Insertion

Accessory carpal bone and the base of the fifth metacarpal.

## Action

Abducts the paw (bends the carpus laterally) and steadies the carpus.

## Innervation

Radial nerve (*n. radialis*).
