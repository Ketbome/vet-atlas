## Overview

In the cat it is a single broad sheet covering the cranial border and the cranial half of the medial surface of the thigh. In the dog, by contrast, it has two separate parts.

## Origin

Crest and cranial ventral iliac spine of the ilium.

## Insertion

Patella and cranial border of the tibia, on its medial side, through the medial femoral fascia.

## Action

Adducts the thigh, flexes the hip and helps extend the stifle.

## Innervation

Femoral nerve (*n. femoralis*), through the saphenous nerve.

## Clinical relevance

Its caudal border forms the cranial border of the **femoral triangle**, where the femoral pulse is palpated.

In the cat, an absent or weak femoral pulse is a key sign of **aortic thromboembolism**.
