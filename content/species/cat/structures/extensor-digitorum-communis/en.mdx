## Overview

Its belly lies caudolateral to the extensor carpi radialis. Its tendon crosses the dorsal surface of the carpus and splits into four, one for each weight-bearing digit.

Claw retraction at rest does not depend on this muscle. **Elastic dorsal ligaments**, running from the middle to the distal phalanx, keep the claw sheathed. The claw is protruded when the deep digital flexor contracts.

## Origin

Lateral epicondyle of the humerus.

## Insertion

Middle and distal phalanges of digits II to V, on their dorsal surface.

## Action

Extends the digital joints and the carpus.

## Innervation

Radial nerve (*n. radialis*).

## Clinical relevance

- With a radial nerve injury the cat cannot extend its digits and knuckles over onto the dorsum of the paw.
- Onychectomy (declawing) amputates the distal phalanx and with it the insertion of this tendon. It is banned in many countries and opposed by veterinary organisations.
