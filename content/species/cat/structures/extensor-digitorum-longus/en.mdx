---
title: Extensor digitorum longus
summary: Digital extensor on the craniolateral surface of the crus, deep to the tibialis cranialis.
keyPoints:
  - It arises from the **extensor fossa** of the femur.
  - Its tendon divides into four for **digits II to V**.
  - It **extends the digits** and flexes the tarsus.
  - It is supplied by the **fibular nerve**.
---

## Overview

Its origin tendon crosses the stifle joint and runs in the extensor groove of the tibia. The belly lies craniolateral on the crus, partly covered by the tibialis cranialis.

## Origin

Extensor fossa of the lateral femoral condyle.

## Insertion

Distal phalanges of digits II to V.

## Action

Extends the digits and flexes the tarsus.

## Innervation

Common fibular (peroneal) nerve (*n. fibularis communis*).
