---
title: Obliquus capitis cranialis
summary: Short muscle between the wing of the atlas and the back of the skull.
keyPoints:
  - It runs from the **wing of the atlas** to the occipital bone.
  - With the other short muscles of the head it moves the **atlanto-occipital joint**.
  - It **extends** the head and tilts it to its own side.
  - It is supplied by the **first cervical nerve**.
---

## Overview

One of the short muscles that move the head on the first two vertebrae. It lies deep to the larger neck muscles that reach the skull.

## Origin

Lateral border and ventral surface of the wing of the atlas.

## Insertion

Nuchal crest and mastoid region of the occipital bone.

## Action

Extends the atlanto-occipital joint. Acting alone, it tilts the head to its own side.

## Innervation

Dorsal branch of the first cervical nerve.
