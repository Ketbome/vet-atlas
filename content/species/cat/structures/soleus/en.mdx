---
title: Soleus
summary: Flat muscle on the caudolateral crus that joins the gastrocnemius tendon; present in the cat, not in the dog.
keyPoints:
  - It arises from the **head of the fibula**.
  - Its tendon joins that of the **gastrocnemius** in the common calcanean tendon.
  - It **extends the tarsus**.
  - It is supplied by the **tibial nerve**.
---

## Overview

It lies lateral to and partly under the lateral head of the gastrocnemius. The cat has a well-developed soleus; in the dog it is absent.

## Origin

Head and proximal part of the fibula.

## Insertion

Calcaneal tuber, through the common calcanean tendon.

## Action

Extends the tarsus.

## Innervation

Tibial nerve (*n. tibialis*).

## Clinical relevance

As part of the common calcanean tendon, it is involved when that tendon is torn or cut, which lets the hock sink.
