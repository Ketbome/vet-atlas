## Overview

It lies in the infraspinous fossa, caudal to the spine of the scapula. The scapular part of the deltoideus partly covers it.

Its tendon glides over a synovial bursa before inserting.

## Origin

Infraspinous fossa (*fossa infraspinata*) of the scapula.

## Insertion

Lateral surface of the greater tubercle of the humerus, distal to the supraspinatus insertion.

## Action

- Abducts the arm and rotates it laterally.
- Depending on the position of the shoulder, it helps flex or extend it.
- It also works as a **lateral collateral ligament** of the joint.

## Innervation

Suprascapular nerve (*n. suprascapularis*).

## Clinical relevance

- Suprascapular nerve injury causes atrophy of the supraspinatus and infraspinatus. The spine of the scapula is left very prominent.
- Palpating the muscle over the scapula is part of muscle condition scoring in the cat.
