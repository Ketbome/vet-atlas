---
title: Infraspinatus
summary: Fills the infraspinous fossa of the scapula; lateral rotator and stabiliser of the shoulder.
---

## Overview

It lies in the infraspinous fossa, caudal to the spine of the scapula, partly covered by the scapular part of the deltoideus. Its tendon glides over a synovial bursa before inserting.

## Origin

Infraspinous fossa (*fossa infraspinata*) of the scapula.

## Insertion

Lateral surface of the greater tubercle of the humerus, distal to the supraspinatus insertion.

## Action

Abducts and rotates the arm laterally. Depending on the position of the shoulder it helps flex or extend it, and it also works as a lateral collateral ligament of the joint.

## Innervation

Suprascapular nerve (*n. suprascapularis*).

## Clinical relevance

- Suprascapular nerve injury causes atrophy of the supraspinatus and infraspinatus, leaving the spine of the scapula very prominent.
- Palpating the muscle over the scapula is part of muscle condition scoring in the cat.
