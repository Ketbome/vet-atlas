---
title: Fibularis longus
summary: Muscle of the lateral crus whose tendon crosses the sole of the tarsus.
keyPoints:
  - It arises from the **lateral condyle of the tibia** and the head of the fibula.
  - Its tendon turns round the lateral malleolus and crosses the **plantar surface of the tarsus**.
  - It **flexes the tarsus** and rotates the paw.
  - It is supplied by the **fibular nerve**.
---

## Overview

A short belly on the lateral surface of the crus, between the long digital extensor and the deep flexors. Its long tendon runs in a groove of the lateral malleolus and then medially across the plantar surface of the tarsus.

## Origin

Lateral condyle of the tibia, head of the fibula and lateral collateral ligament of the stifle.

## Insertion

Fourth tarsal bone and the bases of the metatarsal bones, after crossing the plantar surface of the tarsus.

## Action

Rotates the paw medially (pronation) and flexes the tarsus.

## Innervation

Common fibular (peroneal) nerve (*n. fibularis communis*).
