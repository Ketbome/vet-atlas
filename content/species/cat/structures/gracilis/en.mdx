## Overview

It covers the caudal half of the medial surface of the thigh. The sartorius covers the cranial half.

## Origin

Pelvic symphysis, through the symphyseal tendon.

## Insertion

Medial surface of the proximal tibia, through an aponeurosis continuous with the crural fascia.

## Action

Adducts the pelvic limb, extends the hip and flexes the stifle.

## Innervation

Obturator nerve (*n. obturatorius*).

## Clinical relevance

Fibrotic myopathy of the caudal thigh muscles is known in German Shepherd dogs. In the cat it is only exceptionally reported.
