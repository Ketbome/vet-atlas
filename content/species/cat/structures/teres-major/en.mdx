keyPoints:
  - It runs from the **caudal angle of the scapula** to the medial surface of the humerus.

  - It is supplied by the **axillary nerve**.

## Overview

A spindle-shaped muscle along the caudal border of the scapula, caudal to the subscapularis and medial to the long head of the triceps.

## Origin

Caudal angle and adjacent caudal border of the scapula.

## Insertion

Teres major tuberosity on the medial surface of the humerus, together with the latissimus dorsi.

## Action

Flexes the shoulder joint and draws the humerus caudally.

## Innervation

Axillary nerve (*n. axillaris*).
