---
title: Supraspinatus
summary: Fills the supraspinous fossa of the scapula and extends the shoulder.
---

## Overview

A thick muscle that fills the supraspinous fossa and bulges beyond the cranial border of the scapula. Together with the infraspinatus it acts as a contractile ligament stabilising the shoulder joint.

## Origin

Supraspinous fossa (*fossa supraspinata*) and spine of the scapula.

## Insertion

Greater tubercle of the humerus (*tuberculum majus*).

## Action

Extends and stabilises the shoulder.

## Innervation

Suprascapular nerve (*n. suprascapularis*).

## Clinical relevance

Suprascapular nerve injury, for example in brachial plexus injuries after a road accident, causes atrophy of the supraspinatus and infraspinatus and leaves the spine of the scapula very prominent.
