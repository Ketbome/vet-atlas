## Overview

It is enclosed in the rectus sheath, formed by the aponeuroses of the oblique and transverse abdominal muscles.

The two recti are separated by the linea alba.

## Origin

Sternum and the cartilages of the first ribs.

## Insertion

Pecten of the pubis, through the prepubic tendon.

## Action

Compresses the abdomen and flexes the vertebral column.

## Innervation

Ventral branches of the caudal thoracic and cranial lumbar nerves.

## Clinical relevance

- **Ventral midline laparotomy** goes through the linea alba, between the two recti. It is one of the approaches for ovariohysterectomy in the queen, along with the flank approach.
- The **prepubic tendon** can rupture after trauma.
