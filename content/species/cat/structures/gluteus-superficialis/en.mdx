---
title: Gluteus superficialis
summary: Thin, flat muscle of the croup, caudal to the gluteus medius.
---

## Overview

Classic feline anatomy texts call it gluteus maximus, although in the cat it is a small, thin muscle. The caudofemoralis lies caudal to it.

## Origin

Last sacral vertebrae, first caudal vertebrae and gluteal fascia.

## Insertion

Greater trochanter of the femur, on the caudolateral side of its base; some of its fibres continue into the fascia lata.

## Action

Extends the hip and abducts the pelvic limb.

## Innervation

Caudal gluteal nerve (*n. gluteus caudalis*).
