---
title: Brachialis
summary: Elbow flexor that spirals down the lateral side of the humerus.
---

## Overview

It arises on the caudolateral surface of the proximal humerus and spirals down the brachial groove (*sulcus musculi brachialis*) to the cranial side of the elbow. In the cat it ends on the ulna, together with the tendon of the cleidobrachialis.

## Origin

Caudolateral surface of the proximal humerus, distal to the head.

## Insertion

Proximal end of the ulna, on its medial side, just distal to the trochlear notch.

## Action

Flexes the elbow.

## Innervation

Musculocutaneous nerve (*n. musculocutaneus*).

## Clinical relevance

In the distal half of the arm the radial nerve crosses its lateral surface, so the nerve can be injured in fractures of the humeral shaft.
