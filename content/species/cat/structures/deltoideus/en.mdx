---
title: Deltoideus
summary: Muscle on the lateral side of the shoulder; flexes the shoulder and abducts the arm.
---

## Overview

Classic feline anatomy texts describe three deltoids: clavodeltoid, acromiodeltoid and spinodeltoid. The clavodeltoid is the cleidobrachialis, which veterinary nomenclature places in the brachiocephalicus. The other two are the parts of the deltoideus:

- **Scapular part (*pars scapularis*)**: the spinodeltoid. It arises from the spine of the scapula and covers part of the infraspinatus.
- **Acromial part (*pars acromialis*)**: the acromiodeltoid, shorter, arising from the acromion.

## Origin

Spine of the scapula (scapular part) and acromion (acromial part).

## Insertion

Deltoid tuberosity (*tuberositas deltoidea*) of the humerus.

## Action

Flexes the shoulder and abducts the arm.

## Innervation

Axillary nerve (*n. axillaris*).
