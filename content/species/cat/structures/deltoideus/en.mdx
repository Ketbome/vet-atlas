## Overview

Classic feline anatomy texts describe three deltoids: clavodeltoid, acromiodeltoid and spinodeltoid.

The clavodeltoid is the cleidobrachialis, which veterinary nomenclature places in the brachiocephalicus. The other two are the parts of the deltoideus:

- **Scapular part** (*pars scapularis*). The spinodeltoid. It arises from the spine of the scapula and covers part of the infraspinatus.
- **Acromial part** (*pars acromialis*). The acromiodeltoid, shorter, arising from the acromion.

## Origin

- Scapular part: spine of the scapula.
- Acromial part: acromion.

## Insertion

Deltoid tuberosity (*tuberositas deltoidea*) of the humerus.

## Action

Flexes the shoulder and abducts the arm.

## Innervation

Axillary nerve (*n. axillaris*).
