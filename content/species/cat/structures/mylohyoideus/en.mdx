## Overview

The left and right muscles form a sling between the two halves of the mandible, ventral to the tongue. It is the most superficial muscle between the two halves of the mandible; the digastricus runs along its lateral border.

## Origin

Medial surface of the body of the mandible.

## Insertion

Median fibrous raphe, shared with the muscle of the other side, and the basihyoid bone.

## Action

Raises the floor of the mouth and the tongue, as in the first stage of swallowing.

## Innervation

Mylohyoid nerve, from the mandibular nerve (cranial nerve V).
