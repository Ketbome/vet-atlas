---
title: Pronator teres
summary: Oblique muscle on the craniomedial side of the elbow, from the humerus to the radius.
keyPoints:
  - It runs from the **medial epicondyle** of the humerus to the medial border of the radius.
  - It **pronates** the forearm, turning the palm of the paw back toward the ground.
  - It also helps **flex the elbow**.
  - It is supplied by the **median nerve**.
---

## Overview

The most cranial muscle of the medial group of the forearm. It crosses the flexor surface of the elbow obliquely, distal to the insertion of the biceps brachii.

## Origin

Medial epicondyle of the humerus.

## Insertion

Medial border of the radius, in its proximal half.

## Action

Pronates the forearm and helps flex the elbow.

## Innervation

Median nerve (*n. medianus*).
