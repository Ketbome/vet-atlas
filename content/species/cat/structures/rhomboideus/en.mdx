## Overview

It lies deep to the trapezius. Feline texts often describe its parts as separate muscles: the occipitoscapularis (rhomboideus capitis), the rhomboideus cervicis and the rhomboideus thoracis.

## Origin

- **Head part** (*m. rhomboideus capitis*). Nuchal crest of the occipital bone.
- **Cervical part** (*m. rhomboideus cervicis*). Dorsal midline of the neck.
- **Thoracic part** (*m. rhomboideus thoracis*). Spinous processes of the first thoracic vertebrae.

## Insertion

Dorsal border of the scapula and adjacent medial surface.

## Action

Holds the scapula against the trunk and draws it dorsally and cranially. With the limb fixed, the head part helps raise the head.

## Innervation

Ventral branches of the cervical and first thoracic nerves.
