---
title: Popliteus
summary: Triangular muscle on the caudal surface of the stifle and proximal tibia.
---

## Origin

Lateral condyle of the femur, by a tendon that contains a small sesamoid bone.

## Insertion

Medial part of the caudal surface of the proximal tibia.

## Action

Flexes the stifle and rotates the leg medially.

## Innervation

Tibial nerve (*n. tibialis*).

## Clinical relevance

The sesamoid in its tendon shows on radiographs caudolateral to the stifle and should not be mistaken for a fracture fragment.
