---
title: Sternocephalicus
summary: Long muscle of the ventrolateral neck, from the sternum to the head.
---

## Overview

It has a mastoid part and an occipital part.

## Origin

Manubrium of the sternum.

## Insertion

Mastoid process of the temporal bone (mastoid part) and nuchal crest of the occipital bone (occipital part).

## Action

Draws the head and neck ventrally or to the same side.

## Innervation

Ventral branch of the accessory nerve (*n. accessorius*).

## Clinical relevance

It forms the ventral border of the jugular groove, used to locate and puncture the external jugular vein.
