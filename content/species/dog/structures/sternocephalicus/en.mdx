  - It has two parts, **mastoid and occipital**.

## Overview

It has two parts, mastoid and occipital.

## Origin

Manubrium of the sternum.

## Insertion

- **Mastoid part**. Mastoid process of the temporal bone.
- **Occipital part**. Nuchal crest of the occipital bone.

## Action

Draws the head and neck ventrally or to the same side.

## Innervation

Ventral branch of the accessory nerve (*n. accessorius*).

## Clinical relevance

It forms the ventral border of the jugular groove. The groove is used to locate and puncture the external jugular vein.
