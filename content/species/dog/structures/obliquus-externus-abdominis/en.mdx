## Overview

It has a costal and a lumbar part.

Its fleshy part covers the lateral abdominal wall and the caudoventral thorax. Ventrally it continues as an aponeurosis that reaches the linea alba. The model shows the fleshy part.

## Origin

- **Costal part**. Lateral surfaces of the ribs from the fourth or fifth to the last, by digitations alternating with those of the serratus ventralis.
- **Lumbar part**. Thoracolumbar fascia.

## Insertion

Through its aponeurosis, on the linea alba, prepubic tendon and inguinal ligament.

## Action

Compresses the abdomen (expiration, defecation, urination, parturition) and bends the trunk to the same side.

## Innervation

Ventral branches of the caudal thoracic and cranial lumbar nerves.

## Clinical relevance

Its aponeurosis contains the **superficial inguinal ring**, through which inguinal hernias can protrude.
