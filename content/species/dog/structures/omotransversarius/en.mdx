## Overview

A flat, elongated muscle of the neck. It lies between the trapezius, dorsally, and the brachiocephalicus, ventrally.

It is one of the muscles that attach the thoracic limb to the trunk.

## Origin

Distal part of the spine of the scapula and acromion.

## Insertion

Wing of the atlas, on the caudal border of the transverse process.

## Action

Advances the thoracic limb. With the limb fixed, bends the neck to the same side.

## Innervation

Accessory nerve (*n. accessorius*).

## Clinical relevance

Together with the brachiocephalicus it covers the superficial cervical lymph nodes, which are palpated cranial to the scapula.
