---
title: Omotransversarius
summary: Strap-like muscle from the distal spine of the scapula to the wing of the atlas.
---

## Overview

A flat, elongated muscle of the neck lying between the trapezius (dorsally) and the brachiocephalicus (ventrally). It is one of the muscles that attach the thoracic limb to the trunk.

## Origin

Distal part of the spine of the scapula and acromion.

## Insertion

Wing of the atlas (caudal border of the transverse process).

## Action

Advances the thoracic limb. With the limb fixed, bends the neck to the same side.

## Innervation

Accessory nerve (*n. accessorius*).

## Clinical relevance

Together with the brachiocephalicus it covers the superficial cervical lymph nodes, which are palpated cranial to the scapula.
