---
title: Semimembranosus
summary: Bulky muscle of the caudomedial thigh.
---

## Overview

It lies medial to the semitendinosus and divides into two bellies, one to the femur and one to the tibia.

## Origin

Ventral part of the ischiatic tuberosity (*tuber ischiadicum*).

## Insertion

- Cranial belly: medial lip of the facies aspera, near the medial condyle of the femur.
- Caudal belly: medial condyle of the tibia.

## Action

Extends the hip; depending on the position of the stifle it helps extend or flex it.

## Innervation

Sciatic nerve (*n. ischiadicus*).
