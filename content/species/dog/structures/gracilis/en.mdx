title: Gracilis
summary: Broad, flat muscle of the medial thigh.
keyPoints:
  - It arises from the **pelvic symphysis** through the symphyseal tendon.
  - Through its share of the **common calcanean tendon** it reaches the calcaneal tuber and extends the tarsus.
  - It adducts the limb, extends the hip and **flexes the stifle**.
  - It is supplied by the **obturator nerve**.
  - It can develop **fibrotic contracture** in working dogs, mainly German Shepherd dogs.

## Origin

Pelvic symphysis, through the symphyseal tendon.

## Insertion

- Cranial border of the tibia, on its medial side.
- Calcaneal tuber, through its contribution to the common calcanean tendon.

## Action

Adducts the pelvic limb, extends the hip, flexes the stifle and extends the tarsus.

## Innervation

Obturator nerve (*n. obturatorius*).

## Clinical relevance

Like the semitendinosus, it can develop **fibrotic contracture** in working dogs, mainly German Shepherd dogs.
