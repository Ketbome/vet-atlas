---
title: Serratus ventralis
summary: Large fan-shaped muscle that suspends the trunk between the thoracic limbs.
---

## Overview

It has a cervical and a thoracic part. It is the main component of the thoracic limb synsarcosis: since the dog has no functional clavicle, the trunk hangs between the two scapulae, held by these muscles.

## Origin

- Cervical part: transverse processes of the last five cervical vertebrae.
- Thoracic part: first seven or eight ribs, by digitations.

## Insertion

Serrated face (*facies serrata*), on the dorsal part of the medial surface of the scapula.

## Action

Supports the trunk and moves the scapula. The cervical part draws the dorsal angle of the scapula cranially and the thoracic part caudally. With the limb fixed, the thoracic part assists inspiration.

## Innervation

Ventral branches of the cervical nerves (cervical part) and long thoracic nerve (*n. thoracicus longus*) for the thoracic part.
