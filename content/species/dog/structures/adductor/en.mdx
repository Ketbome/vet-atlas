---
title: Adductor
summary: Large muscle of the medial thigh, deep to the gracilis.
---

## Overview

In the dog it has a large part (*m. adductor magnus et brevis*) and a small cranial part (*m. adductor longus*). The model shows both as one mesh.

## Origin

Pelvic symphysis, through the symphyseal tendon, and the ventral surface of the pubis and ischium.

## Insertion

Lateral lip of the caudal surface of the femur, along most of its length.

## Action

Adducts the limb and extends the hip.

## Innervation

Obturator nerve (*n. obturatorius*).

## Clinical relevance

With the pectineus and gracilis it depends on the obturator nerve. When that nerve is injured, for example in pelvic fractures, the limb slides outward on slippery floors.
