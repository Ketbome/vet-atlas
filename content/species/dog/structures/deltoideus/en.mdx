---
title: Deltoideus
summary: Shoulder flexor lying on the lateral surface of the scapula and proximal humerus.
keyPoints:
  - Its **scapular part** and acromial part converge on the deltoid tuberosity.
  - The scapular part covers the **infraspinatus**.
  - It flexes the shoulder and **abducts** the arm.
  - It is supplied by the **axillary nerve**.
---

## Overview

It has two parts converging on the deltoid tuberosity:

- **Scapular part** (*pars scapularis*). Arises by an aponeurosis from the spine of the scapula and covers the infraspinatus.
- **Acromial part** (*pars acromialis*). Smaller and spindle-shaped. It arises from the acromion.

## Origin

Spine of the scapula (scapular part) and acromion (acromial part).

## Insertion

Deltoid tuberosity (*tuberositas deltoidea*) of the humerus.

## Action

Flexes the shoulder and abducts the arm.

## Innervation

Axillary nerve (*n. axillaris*).
