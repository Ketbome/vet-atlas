---
title: Gluteus superficialis
summary: Flat muscle of the croup, caudal to the gluteus medius, ending on the third trochanter.
keyPoints:
  - It inserts on the **third trochanter** of the femur.
  - Part of its origin is the **sacrotuberous ligament**.
  - It extends the hip and **abducts** the pelvic limb.
  - It is supplied by the **caudal gluteal nerve**.
---

## Origin

- Lateral border of the sacrum and first caudal vertebra.
- Sacrotuberous ligament.
- Gluteal fascia.

## Insertion

Third trochanter of the femur (*trochanter tertius*).

## Action

Extends the hip and abducts the pelvic limb.

## Innervation

Caudal gluteal nerve (*n. gluteus caudalis*).
