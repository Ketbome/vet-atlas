---
title: Gluteus superficialis
summary: Flat muscle of the croup, caudal to the gluteus medius, ending on the third trochanter.
---

## Origin

Lateral border of the sacrum and first caudal vertebra, sacrotuberous ligament and gluteal fascia.

## Insertion

Third trochanter of the femur (*trochanter tertius*).

## Action

Extends the hip and abducts the pelvic limb.

## Innervation

Caudal gluteal nerve (*n. gluteus caudalis*).
