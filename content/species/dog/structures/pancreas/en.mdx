## Topography

- **Body**: in the angle between the pylorus and the cranial duodenal flexure.
- **Right lobe**: in the mesoduodenum, along the descending duodenum, ventral to the right kidney.
- **Left lobe**: in the deep leaf of the greater omentum, caudal to the greater curvature of the stomach, towards the spleen and the cranial pole of the left kidney.

## Ducts

The pancreatic duct opens with the bile duct at the major duodenal papilla; the accessory pancreatic duct, usually the main one in the dog, opens at the minor duodenal papilla.

## Clinical relevance

- **Acute pancreatitis**: cranial abdominal pain ("prayer position"), vomiting; diagnosed with pancreas-specific lipase and ultrasound.
- **Exocrine pancreatic insufficiency**: from acinar atrophy, common in German Shepherd Dogs; causes voluminous diarrhoea and weight loss.
- **Diabetes mellitus and insulinoma**: disorders of the pancreatic islets.
