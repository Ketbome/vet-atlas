## Topography

- **Body**. In the angle between the pylorus and the cranial duodenal flexure.
- **Right lobe**. In the mesoduodenum, along the descending duodenum, ventral to the right kidney.
- **Left lobe**. In the deep leaf of the greater omentum, caudal to the greater curvature of the stomach, towards the spleen and the cranial pole of the left kidney.

## Ducts

- **Pancreatic duct**. Opens with the bile duct at the major duodenal papilla.
- **Accessory pancreatic duct**. Opens at the minor duodenal papilla. In the dog it is usually the main one.

## Clinical relevance

- **Acute pancreatitis**. Cranial abdominal pain, with the "prayer position", and vomiting. It is diagnosed with pancreas-specific lipase and ultrasound.
- **Exocrine pancreatic insufficiency**. Caused by acinar atrophy and common in German Shepherd Dogs. It causes voluminous diarrhoea and weight loss.
- **Diabetes mellitus and insulinoma**. Disorders of the pancreatic islets.
