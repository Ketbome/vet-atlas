---
title: Fibularis longus
summary: Lateral muscle of the leg whose tendon crosses under the tarsus; also called peroneus longus.
---

## Origin

Lateral condyle of the tibia, head of the fibula and lateral collateral ligament of the stifle.

## Insertion

Its tendon passes over the lateral malleolus and crosses the plantar surface of the tarsus to the fourth tarsal bone and the bases of the metatarsal bones.

## Action

Rotates the paw medially (pronation) and flexes the tarsus.

## Innervation

Common fibular (peroneal) nerve (*n. fibularis communis*).
