---
title: Iliopsoas
summary: Main flexor of the hip, running from the lumbar spine and ilium to the lesser trochanter.
---

## Overview

Two muscles that end together: the psoas major (*m. psoas major*) and the iliacus (*m. iliacus*).

## Origin

- **Psoas major**: bodies and transverse processes of the lumbar vertebrae.
- **Iliacus**: ventral surface of the ilium.

## Insertion

Lesser trochanter of the femur.

## Action

Flexes the hip and draws the limb forward. With the limb fixed, it flexes the lumbar spine.

## Innervation

Ventral branches of the lumbar nerves and the femoral nerve (*n. femoralis*).

## Clinical relevance

Strain of the iliopsoas is a cause of pelvic limb lameness in active and working dogs. Extending the hip while rotating it inward stretches the muscle and is painful.
