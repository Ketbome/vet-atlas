---
title: Gluteus profundus
summary: Deepest of the gluteal muscles, a short fan over the hip joint.
---

## Origin

Body of the ilium and ischiatic spine.

## Insertion

Cranial part of the greater trochanter of the femur.

## Action

Extends and abducts the hip and rotates the limb medially.

## Innervation

Cranial gluteal nerve (*n. gluteus cranialis*).

## Clinical relevance

It lies directly on the joint capsule of the hip, which surgical approaches to the hip reach beneath it.
