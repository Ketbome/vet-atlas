---
title: Obturator externus
summary: Deep hip muscle that covers the obturator foramen from below.
---

## Origin

Ventral surface of the pubis and ischium around the obturator foramen.

## Insertion

Trochanteric fossa of the femur.

## Action

Rotates the limb outward (laterally) at the hip.

## Innervation

Obturator nerve (*n. obturatorius*).
