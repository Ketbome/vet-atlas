---
title: Rectus abdominis
summary: Ventral muscular band on each side of the linea alba, from the sternum to the pubis.
---

## Overview

It is enclosed in the rectus sheath, formed by the aponeuroses of the oblique and transverse abdominal muscles, and is crossed by transverse tendinous intersections.

## Origin

First costal cartilage and sternum.

## Insertion

Pecten of the pubis, through the prepubic tendon.

## Action

Compresses the abdomen and flexes the vertebral column.

## Innervation

Ventral branches of the caudal thoracic and cranial lumbar nerves.

## Clinical relevance

Ventral midline laparotomy goes through the linea alba, between the two recti. The prepubic tendon can rupture after trauma or in late pregnancy.
