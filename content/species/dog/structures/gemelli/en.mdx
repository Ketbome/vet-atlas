---
title: Gemelli
summary: Small deep hip muscles on either side of the tendon of the obturator internus.
---

## Overview

The scan shows a cranial and a caudal belly. In the dog they are often described as a single muscle, grooved by the tendon of the obturator internus.

## Origin

Lateral surface of the ischium, caudal to the acetabulum.

## Insertion

Trochanteric fossa of the femur.

## Action

Rotate the limb outward (laterally) at the hip.

## Innervation

Sciatic nerve (*n. ischiadicus*).
