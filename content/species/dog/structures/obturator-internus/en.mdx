---
title: Obturator internus
summary: Fan-shaped muscle on the floor of the pelvic cavity whose tendon turns around the ischium to the femur.
---

## Origin

Inner (pelvic) surface of the pubis and ischium around the obturator foramen.

## Insertion

Trochanteric fossa of the femur. Its tendon turns over the lesser ischiatic notch, between the gemelli.

## Action

Rotates the limb outward (laterally) at the hip.

## Innervation

Sciatic nerve (*n. ischiadicus*).

## Clinical relevance

In perineal hernia repair it is lifted from the pelvic floor and turned up to close the defect (internal obturator transposition).
