---
title: Extensor carpi radialis
summary: The most cranial muscle of the forearm; extends the carpus.
keyPoints:
  - It arises from the **lateral supracondylar crest** of the humerus.
  - Its two tendons end on the bases of **metacarpals II and III**.
  - It is supplied by the **radial nerve**.
  - Tapping its belly extends the carpus; this is the **extensor carpi radialis reflex**.
---

## Overview

Its belly lies on the cranial surface of the forearm. It continues as two tendons crossing the dorsal surface of the carpus.

## Origin

Lateral supracondylar crest of the humerus.

## Insertion

Dorsal surface of the bases of metacarpal bones II and III.

## Action

Extends the carpus.

## Innervation

Radial nerve (*n. radialis*).

## Clinical relevance

It is used in the neurological examination. Tapping its belly extends the carpus (extensor carpi radialis reflex), which tests the radial nerve.
