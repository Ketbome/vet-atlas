---
title: Extensor carpi radialis
summary: The most cranial muscle of the forearm; extends the carpus.
---

## Overview

Its belly lies on the cranial surface of the forearm and continues as two tendons crossing the dorsal surface of the carpus.

## Origin

Lateral supracondylar crest of the humerus.

## Insertion

Dorsal surface of the bases of metacarpal bones II and III.

## Action

Extends the carpus.

## Innervation

Radial nerve (*n. radialis*).

## Clinical relevance

It is used in the neurological examination: tapping its belly extends the carpus (extensor carpi radialis reflex), which tests the radial nerve.
