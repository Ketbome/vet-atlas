---
title: Flexor carpi ulnaris
summary: Carpal flexor on the caudal surface of the forearm.
---

## Overview

It has two heads: a deep humeral head and a thinner, superficial ulnar head. Both end on the accessory carpal bone.

## Origin

- Humeral head (*caput humerale*): medial epicondyle of the humerus.
- Ulnar head (*caput ulnare*): caudal surface of the olecranon.

## Insertion

Accessory carpal bone (*os carpi accessorium*).

## Action

Flexes the carpus.

## Innervation

Ulnar nerve (*n. ulnaris*).
