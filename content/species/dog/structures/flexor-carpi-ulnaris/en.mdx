## Overview

It has two heads. The humeral head is deep; the ulnar head is superficial and thinner. Both end on the accessory carpal bone.

## Origin

- Humeral head (*caput humerale*), from the medial epicondyle of the humerus.
- Ulnar head (*caput ulnare*), from the caudal surface of the olecranon.

## Insertion

Accessory carpal bone (*os carpi accessorium*).

## Action

Flexes the carpus.

## Innervation

Ulnar nerve (*n. ulnaris*).
