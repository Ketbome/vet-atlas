---
title: Semitendinosus
summary: Long muscle forming the caudal border of the thigh, between the biceps femoris and the semimembranosus.
---

## Origin

Ischiatic tuberosity (*tuber ischiadicum*).

## Insertion

Medial surface of the proximal tibia and, by a tarsal tendon joining the common calcanean tendon, the calcaneal tuber.

## Action

Extends the hip, flexes the stifle and extends the tarsus.

## Innervation

Sciatic nerve (*n. ischiadicus*).

## Clinical relevance

Fibrotic myopathy of the semitendinosus, described mainly in German Shepherd dogs, shortens the stride of the pelvic limb, with a quick medial flick of the paw at the end of the swing phase.
