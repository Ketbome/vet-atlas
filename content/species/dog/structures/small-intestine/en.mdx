## Parts

### Duodenum

It starts at the pylorus and has these segments:

- cranial part
- cranial flexure
- descending duodenum, along the right side of the abdomen
- caudal flexure, near the pelvic inlet
- ascending duodenum, left of the root of the mesentery

It ends at the duodenojejunal flexure.

### Jejunum

The longest part. Its loops are mobile, hang from the mesentery and fill the middle and ventral abdomen.

### Ileum

The short final segment. It opens into the colon at the ileocolic orifice, on the right, at the level of the first lumbar vertebrae.

## Clinical relevance

- **Foreign body obstruction**. Very common. Linear foreign bodies plicate the intestine.
- **Intussusception**. Mostly ileocolic in puppies, often secondary to enteritis.
- **Parvoviral enteritis**. Haemorrhagic diarrhoea in unvaccinated puppies.
- **Ultrasound**. Measures wall thickness and layering in chronic enteropathies.
