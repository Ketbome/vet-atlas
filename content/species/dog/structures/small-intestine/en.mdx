---
title: Small intestine
summary: Part of the gut between the pylorus and the ileocolic orifice. Its loops, mainly jejunal, fill most of the ventral abdomen.
---

## Parts

- **Duodenum**: cranial part from the pylorus, cranial flexure, descending duodenum along the right side of the abdomen, caudal flexure near the pelvic inlet, and ascending duodenum left of the root of the mesentery, up to the duodenojejunal flexure.
- **Jejunum**: the longest part; its mobile loops, hung from the mesentery, fill the middle and ventral abdomen.
- **Ileum**: short final segment opening into the colon at the ileocolic orifice, on the right, at the level of the first lumbar vertebrae.

## Clinical relevance

- **Foreign body obstruction**: very common; linear foreign bodies plicate the intestine.
- **Intussusception**: mostly ileocolic in puppies, often secondary to enteritis.
- **Parvoviral enteritis**: haemorrhagic diarrhoea in unvaccinated puppies.
- **Ultrasound**: measures wall thickness and layering in chronic enteropathies.
