---
title: Sartorius
summary: Strap-like muscle of the cranial border and medial surface of the thigh, in two parts.
---

## Overview

In the dog it has two separate parts: a cranial part forming the cranial contour of the thigh, and a caudal part on the medial surface.

## Origin

- Cranial part: crest of the ilium and thoracolumbar fascia.
- Caudal part: cranial ventral iliac spine and the adjacent ventral border of the ilium.

## Insertion

- Cranial part: patella, through the medial femoral fascia.
- Caudal part: cranial border of the tibia, on its medial side.

## Action

Flexes the hip and adducts the limb. The cranial part extends the stifle and the caudal part flexes it.

## Innervation

Femoral nerve (*n. femoralis*), through the saphenous nerve.

## Clinical relevance

The caudal part of the sartorius forms the cranial border of the femoral triangle, where the femoral pulse is palpated.
