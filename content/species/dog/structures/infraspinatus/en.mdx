## Overview

It lies in the infraspinous fossa, caudal to the spine of the scapula. It is partly covered by the scapular part of the deltoideus.

Its tendon glides over a synovial bursa before inserting.

## Origin

Infraspinous fossa (*fossa infraspinata*) of the scapula.

## Insertion

Lateral surface of the greater tubercle of the humerus, distal to the supraspinatus insertion.

## Action

Abducts and rotates the arm laterally. Depending on the position of the shoulder, it helps flex or extend it.

It also works as a lateral collateral ligament of the joint.

## Innervation

Suprascapular nerve (*n. suprascapularis*).

## Clinical relevance

**Infraspinatus contracture** is typical of working and hunting dogs. It produces a characteristic gait, with the elbow adducted and the forearm rotated outward.
