## Topography

They arise in each lung lobe and leave at the hilus.

They open, as several trunks, into the roof of the left atrium on the dorsocaudal aspect of the heart base. The number of openings varies between dogs.

On a dorsoventral radiograph the caudal lobar veins are medial to their arteries.

## Clinical relevance

In **left-sided heart failure**, from myxomatous mitral valve disease or dilated cardiomyopathy, left atrial pressure distends the pulmonary veins.

On radiographs they look wider than their arteries before pulmonary oedema appears.

## 3D model

Segmented from the heart CT. They are the medial vascular tree converging on the left atrium, and they continue inside the lungs.
