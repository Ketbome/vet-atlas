## Overview

It has a lateral and a medial head. The tendon of origin of each contains a sesamoid bone, the **fabella**.

Their tendons form the main part of the common calcanean tendon.

## Origin

Lateral and medial supracondylar tuberosities of the femur.

## Insertion

Calcaneal tuber (*tuber calcanei*).

## Action

Extends the tarsus and flexes the stifle.

## Innervation

Tibial nerve (*n. tibialis*).

## Clinical relevance

Rupture or avulsion of the **common calcanean tendon** makes the hock drop. The dog stands with the heel near the ground, in a plantigrade stance.

The common calcanean tendon includes:

- the gastrocnemius
- the superficial digital flexor
- the tarsal tendons of the biceps femoris, semitendinosus and gracilis
