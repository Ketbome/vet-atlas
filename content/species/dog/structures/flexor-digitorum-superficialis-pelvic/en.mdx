---
title: Superficial digital flexor of the pelvic limb
summary: Digital flexor whose tendon caps the calcaneal tuber.
---

## Overview

It arises deep, between the two heads of the gastrocnemius. Its tendon winds around the gastrocnemius tendon, becomes superficial, forms a cap over the calcaneal tuber held by retinacula, and continues along the plantar surface of the metatarsus.

## Origin

Lateral supracondylar tuberosity of the femur.

## Insertion

Middle phalanges of digits II to V.

## Action

Flexes the digits and extends the tarsus.

## Innervation

Tibial nerve (*n. tibialis*).

## Clinical relevance

If the retinaculum holding it on the calcaneus tears, the tendon luxates, usually laterally. It is described most often in Shetland Sheepdogs and Collies.
