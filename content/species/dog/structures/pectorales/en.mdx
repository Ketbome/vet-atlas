---
title: Pectoral muscles
summary: Muscles of the ventral thorax joining the sternum to the humerus; adductors of the thoracic limb.
---

## Overview

The dog has two groups:

- **Superficial pectorals (*mm. pectorales superficiales*)**: the descending and transverse pectorals, cranial and superficial.
- **Deep pectoral (*m. pectoralis profundus*)**: larger, caudal and deep to the former.

## Origin

- Superficial pectorals: manubrium and first sternebrae.
- Deep pectoral: ventral surface of the sternum and, caudally, the deep abdominal fascia.

## Insertion

- Superficial pectorals: crest of the greater tubercle of the humerus; the transverse pectoral continues into the antebrachial fascia.
- Deep pectoral: mainly the lesser tubercle of the humerus, with a sheet reaching the greater tubercle.

## Action

Adduct the thoracic limb. The deep pectoral also draws the limb caudally and, with the limb planted, pulls the trunk cranially.

## Innervation

Cranial and caudal pectoral nerves (*nn. pectorales*) from the brachial plexus.
