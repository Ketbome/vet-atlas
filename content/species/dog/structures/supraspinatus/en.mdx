## Overview

A thick muscle that fills the supraspinous fossa and bulges beyond the cranial border of the scapula.

Together with the infraspinatus it acts as a contractile ligament stabilising the shoulder joint.

## Origin

Supraspinous fossa (*fossa supraspinata*), spine and cranial border of the scapula.

## Insertion

Greater tubercle of the humerus (*tuberculum majus*).

## Action

Extends and stabilises the shoulder.

## Innervation

Suprascapular nerve (*n. suprascapularis*).

## Clinical relevance

- **Tendinopathy** and **mineralisation** of the supraspinatus tendon are recognised causes of forelimb lameness in medium and large breeds.
- **Suprascapular nerve injury** causes atrophy of the supraspinatus and infraspinatus. The spine of the scapula becomes very prominent.
