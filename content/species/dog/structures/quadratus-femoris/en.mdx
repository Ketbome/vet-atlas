---
title: Quadratus femoris
summary: Short, thick muscle between the ischium and the proximal femur.
---

## Origin

Ventral surface of the ischium.

## Insertion

Caudal surface of the femur, just distal to the trochanteric fossa.

## Action

Extends the hip and rotates the limb outward (laterally).

## Innervation

Sciatic nerve (*n. ischiadicus*).
