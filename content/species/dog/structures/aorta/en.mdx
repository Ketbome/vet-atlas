## Topography

- **Ascending aorta**. A short segment leaving the left ventricle in the centre of the heart base. The coronary arteries arise from the aortic bulb.
- **Aortic arch** (*arcus aortae*). Curves dorsally and caudally to the left of the trachea and esophagus. It gives off the brachiocephalic trunk and the left subclavian artery.
- **Descending thoracic aorta**. Runs in the dorsal mediastinum, ventral to the vertebral bodies and slightly left of the median plane. It gives off the dorsal intercostal arteries.
- **Abdominal aorta**. Crosses the diaphragm through the aortic hiatus, between the crura. It ends at the last lumbar vertebrae.

### Branches of the abdominal aorta

- coeliac artery
- cranial mesenteric artery
- renal arteries
- gonadal arteries
- caudal mesenteric artery

At its end it divides into the external and internal iliac arteries and the median sacral artery.

## Clinical relevance

- **Patent ductus arteriosus**. The fetal duct joining the aortic arch to the pulmonary trunk fails to close. It causes a continuous murmur at the left heart base.
- **Subaortic stenosis**. A common congenital defect in large breeds: Newfoundland, Golden Retriever, Rottweiler and Boxer.
- **Persistent right aortic arch**. A vascular ring that constricts the esophagus and causes regurgitation in puppies at weaning.
- **Aortic thromboembolism**. Uncommon in dogs. It causes acute paresis of the pelvic limbs.
