---
title: Pectineus
summary: Small spindle-shaped muscle of the medial thigh, between the sartorius and the adductor.
---

## Origin

Iliopubic eminence and pecten of the pubis.

## Insertion

Distal end of the femur, on the medial part of its caudal surface.

## Action

Adducts the limb and helps flex the hip.

## Innervation

Obturator nerve (*n. obturatorius*).

## Clinical relevance

It is easy to feel on the medial thigh. Cutting it (pectineal myotomy) has been used to relieve pain in hip dysplasia; it does not change the course of the disease.
