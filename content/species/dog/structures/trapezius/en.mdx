---
title: Trapezius
summary: Thin triangular sheet joining the dorsal midline of the neck and withers to the spine of the scapula.
---

## Overview

The most superficial muscle of the scapular region. It has a cervical part (*pars cervicalis*) and a thoracic part (*pars thoracica*), joined by a tendinous strip at the withers. Both converge on the spine of the scapula.

## Origin

Median fibrous raphe of the neck and supraspinous ligament, from about the third cervical to the ninth thoracic vertebra.

## Insertion

Spine of the scapula (*spina scapulae*).

## Action

Elevates and abducts the thoracic limb. The cervical part draws the scapula craniodorsally, the thoracic part caudodorsally.

## Innervation

Dorsal branch of the accessory nerve (*n. accessorius*, cranial nerve XI).
