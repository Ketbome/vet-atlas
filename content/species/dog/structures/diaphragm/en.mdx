## Overview

It is convex cranially. Its periphery is muscular and its centre is a tendon (*centrum tendineum*).

It has three openings:

- the aortic hiatus
- the oesophageal hiatus
- the caval foramen

## Origin

- **Lumbar part**. Right and left crura, on the bodies of the cranial lumbar vertebrae (L3–L4).
- **Costal part**. Medial surfaces of the eighth to thirteenth ribs, along the costal arch.
- **Sternal part**. Xiphoid cartilage.

## Insertion

Central tendon.

## Action

Contraction flattens the dome, enlarges the thorax and produces inspiration.

## Innervation

Phrenic nerve (*n. phrenicus*), formed by ventral branches of cervical nerves **C5 to C7**.

## Clinical relevance

- **Traumatic diaphragmatic hernia**, usually after road accidents, displaces abdominal organs into the thorax.
- Peritoneopericardial diaphragmatic hernia is congenital.
- Hiatal hernia is described mostly in brachycephalic breeds and the Shar-Pei.
