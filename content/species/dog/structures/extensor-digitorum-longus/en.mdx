---
title: Long digital extensor
summary: Craniolateral muscle of the leg that extends the toes.
---

## Origin

Extensor fossa of the lateral condyle of the femur. Its tendon of origin runs through the stifle joint and the extensor groove of the tibia.

## Insertion

Distal phalanges of digits II to V.

## Action

Extends the digits and flexes the tarsus.

## Innervation

Common fibular (peroneal) nerve (*n. fibularis communis*).

## Clinical relevance

In young dogs its tendon of origin can be torn off the femur (avulsion), causing stifle lameness.
