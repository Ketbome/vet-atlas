---
title: Temporalis
summary: Powerful masticatory muscle that fills the temporal fossa and closes the mouth.
---

## Overview

In the dog it is the largest of the masticatory muscles. It covers the lateral surface of the cranial vault and passes medial to the zygomatic arch to the mandible.

## Origin

Temporal fossa, external sagittal crest and nuchal crest.

## Insertion

Coronoid process of the mandible.

## Action

Elevates the mandible and closes the mouth.

## Innervation

Mandibular nerve (*n. mandibularis*), a branch of the trigeminal nerve (cranial nerve V).

## Clinical relevance

Masticatory muscle myositis is an autoimmune disease against type 2M fibres, which only these muscles have. In the acute phase it causes pain and inability to open the mouth; in the chronic phase, marked atrophy of the temporalis and masseter.
