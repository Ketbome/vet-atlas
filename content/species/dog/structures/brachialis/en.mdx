## Overview

It arises on the caudal surface of the proximal humerus. It spirals down the brachial groove (*sulcus musculi brachialis*) to the cranial side of the elbow.

## Origin

Caudolateral surface of the proximal humerus, distal to the head.

## Insertion

Ulnar and radial tuberosities, on the medial side of the proximal forearm.

## Action

Flexes the elbow.

## Innervation

Musculocutaneous nerve (*n. musculocutaneus*).

## Clinical relevance

In the distal half of the arm the **radial nerve** crosses its lateral surface. The nerve can therefore be injured in fractures of the humeral shaft.
