---
title: Deep digital flexor (pelvic limb)
summary: Deep muscle on the caudal surface of the leg that flexes the toes.
---

## Overview

Two bellies whose tendons join at the tarsus: the lateral digital flexor (*m. flexor digitorum lateralis*), the larger, and the medial digital flexor (*m. flexor digitorum medialis*).

## Origin

Caudal surface of the tibia and fibula and the interosseous membrane between them.

## Insertion

Plantar surface of the distal phalanges of digits II to V.

## Action

Flexes the digits and extends the tarsus.

## Innervation

Tibial nerve (*n. tibialis*).
