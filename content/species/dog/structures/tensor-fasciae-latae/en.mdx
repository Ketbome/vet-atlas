---
title: Tensor fasciae latae
summary: Triangular muscle of the craniolateral thigh that tenses the fascia lata.
---

## Overview

Its fleshy part fills the angle between the gluteus medius and the sartorius. It continues into the fascia lata, the thick lateral fascia of the thigh that reaches the patella.

## Origin

Tuber coxae and the adjacent ventral border of the ilium.

## Insertion

Fascia lata and, through it, the patella.

## Action

Flexes the hip and extends the stifle by tensing the fascia lata.

## Innervation

Cranial gluteal nerve (*n. gluteus cranialis*).
