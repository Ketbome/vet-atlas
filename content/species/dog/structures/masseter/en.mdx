---
title: Masseter
summary: Thick masticatory muscle of the cheek, between the zygomatic arch and the mandible.
keyPoints:
  - It arises from the ventral border and medial surface of the **zygomatic arch**.
  - It inserts in the **masseteric fossa** of the mandible.
  - It elevates the mandible and **closes the mouth**.
  - It is supplied by the masseteric nerve, from the **mandibular nerve** (cranial nerve V).
  - It is affected together with the temporalis in **masticatory muscle myositis**.
---

## Origin

Ventral border and medial surface of the zygomatic arch.

## Insertion

Masseteric fossa and ventral border of the caudal part of the mandible.

## Action

Elevates the mandible and closes the mouth.

## Innervation

Masseteric nerve, from the mandibular nerve (cranial nerve V).

## Clinical relevance

It is affected together with the temporalis in masticatory muscle myositis.
