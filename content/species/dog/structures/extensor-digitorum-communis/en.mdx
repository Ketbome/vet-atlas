---
title: Common digital extensor
summary: Digital extensor on the craniolateral surface of the forearm.
---

## Overview

Its belly lies caudolateral to the extensor carpi radialis. Its tendon crosses the dorsal surface of the carpus and splits into four, one for each weight-bearing digit.

## Origin

Lateral epicondyle of the humerus.

## Insertion

Extensor processes of the distal phalanges of digits II to V.

## Action

Extends the digital joints and the carpus.

## Innervation

Radial nerve (*n. radialis*).

## Clinical relevance

With a radial nerve injury the dog cannot extend its digits and knuckles over onto the dorsum of the paw.
