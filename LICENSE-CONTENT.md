# Content license

Everything under `content/` that was written for this project (structure and
pathology texts, captions, translations) is licensed under
**Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Third-party material keeps its original license. Every image and every 3D model
records its source, author and license in `content/species/*/media.json` and
`models/manifest.json`, and that attribution is displayed next to the item.

The project is non-commercial. This is what allows us to use sources licensed
CC BY-NC (for example the Ohio State *Veterinary Histology* atlas) and
MorphoSource datasets marked "Non-Commercial Use Permitted". Do not add
advertising, paid tiers or any commercial use without re-auditing every source.

Models and images under a "NoDerivs" (ND) license are **not accepted**:
decimating, renaming meshes or cropping is a derivative work.
